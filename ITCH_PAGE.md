## Description (the page body — paste everything below this line)

**Play in Chrome or Firefox. Safari does not run this build correctly.**
**Click once inside the game window to start.**

You are sitting a written exam you did not study for. Your phone is under the
desk. Hold **Space** to look down at it and enter the arrow sequence on screen,
and it gives you the answer to one question — once.

Then you have to carry that answer in your head. Look up, find the row, fill the
bubble. Nothing is written down for you, nothing can be checked again, and the
sequences get longer as the paper gets harder — four arrows on the first
questions, twelve on the last.

The invigilator walks the room. He is not looking for you specifically, which is
the problem: you cannot tell the difference between being watched and being near.
Listen to his footsteps. When they stop, he has stopped.

The errors this game is about are not bad luck. They are yours — the answer you
swapped between two questions, the row you were one off, the sequence you rushed
because he was close. A calm player gets almost all of it right.

### Controls

| | |
|---|---|
| Mouse | Look around |
| Hold Space | Take out your phone |
| Arrow keys (or WASD) | Enter the sequence |
| Right mouse | Lean in over your paper |
| Left click, or 1–4 | Mark an answer |
| Hold left click on HAND IN | Submit the paper early |
| Q | Make a classmate drop a pencil |
| G | Call a classmate (phone must be out) |
| F | Make a classmate shout (once per exam) |
| R | Retake, from the results screen |

**If you memorise a sequence you can enter it straight onto the answer sheet**
without taking your phone out at all — aim at a row and type it. Get it wrong and
it still writes an answer. It just writes the wrong one, and tells you nothing.

### Made in 72 hours

Theme: **Human Error**. Built in Unity 6 by two people.

**Orlando Dylan Tan** — player systems: seated camera, phone and arrow combos,
answer sheet, scoring and results, ambience.
**Theodore** — invigilator: navigation, vision, suspicion, distractions.

Character models by Floreswa and school environment props, both from the Unity
Asset Store. Character animation from Mixamo. Audio by freesound_community,
spinopel, jeremayjimenez, tohubohu and johnnybacon156. Heartbeat and footsteps
are generated procedurally at runtime.
